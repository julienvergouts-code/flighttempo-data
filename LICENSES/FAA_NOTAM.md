# FAA Prohibitions, Restrictions and Notices (PRN)

Provider: Federal Aviation Administration (FAA)  
Source page: https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/us_restrictions/index.cfm

The importer collects the KICZ NOTAM PDF references linked from the FAA PRN page and extracts their text, FIR identifiers and applicability sections. FAA government information is generally public information; retain the source URL and FAA attribution.

**Important limitations:** the PRN page is an index, not a live NOTAM amendment/cancellation service. A KICZ notice may be permanent until cancelled by another NOTAM. The imported records are therefore marked as listed by FAA with currentness unverified. Do not treat this file alone as proof that a restriction remains in force.

KICZ is a NOTAM series/identifier, not a FIR code. The affected FIR codes are extracted from each notice. Applicability is preserved separately (for example, U.S. air carriers, FAA certificate holders or U.S.-registered aircraft); do not apply every notice to every airline or operator.

The app should match the notice's applicability to the relevant operator/pilot/aircraft profile and verify the current NOTAM before treating it as a hard legal route restriction. Pointer NOTAMs are references, not standalone prohibitions.
